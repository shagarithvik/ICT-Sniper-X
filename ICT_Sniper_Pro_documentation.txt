ICT SNIPER PRO - DOCUMENTATION
================================

FILES
-----
ICT_Sniper_Pro_fixed claude 1.pine is the final TradingView Pine Script v6 strategy.
This workspace intentionally contains no Python backend, broker connector, credentials, or live-trading service.

OVERVIEW
--------
The strategy is designed for XAU/GOLD and BTC symbols on chart timeframes of 4H or lower.
It combines confirmed 4H context, daily reference levels, local liquidity sweeps, FVG/IFVG events,
order blocks, premium/discount location, momentum filters, risk-based sizing, and bracket exits.

STRATEGY RULES
--------------
BOS/CHOCH:
- Confirmed local and 4H pivot levels are broken by a closing price.
- A direction change is labeled CHOCH; continuation is labeled BOS.

LIQUIDITY SWEEPS:
- SSL: price wicks below a confirmed swing low and closes above it.
- BSL: price wicks above a confirmed swing high and closes below it.
- A local sweep remains active for the configured sweep window.

FVG/IFVG:
- Bullish FVG: low > high[2] and the gap is at least 0.1 ATR.
- Bearish FVG: high < low[2] and the gap is at least 0.1 ATR.
- FVGs expire after the configured maximum age.
- A bullish FVG becomes bearish IFVG when close falls below its lower boundary.
- A bearish FVG becomes bullish IFVG when close rises above its upper boundary.

ORDER BLOCKS:
- On a confirmed 4H BOS, the latest opposite-color 4H candle is used if it is no more than 10 bars old.

PREMIUM/DISCOUNT:
- The confirmed 100-bar 4H high/low range midpoint is equilibrium.
- Long setups require entry below equilibrium.
- Short setups require entry above equilibrium.

ENTRY ELIGIBILITY:
- Symbol must contain XAU/GOLD or BTC.
- Chart timeframe must be 4H or lower.
- Confirmed data, volume, 4H trend, matching sweep, displacement, risk/reward, location,
  and ATR stop bounds are required.
- Long and short directions can be independently disabled.
- Optional session filtering can block entries outside the configured session.
- Existing position or pending order, daily limits, loss limits, and cooldown block new entries.

CONFLUENCE SCORE:
- Eligibility starts with 3 points for context, signal, displacement, RR, location, and stop validity.
- Extra points may come from MACD/RSI, EMA, VWAP, golden pocket, order block, daily sweep relation,
  local trend, VMC, and Gamma.
- minSc is required. perfSc selects the configured risk multiplier.
- A score cannot override a failed eligibility condition.

RISK AND EXITS
--------------
- EUR risk is selected separately for XAU and BTC.
- Sizing uses the confirmed EURUSD daily reference, stop distance, syminfo.pointvalue,
  and syminfo.mincontract, rounded down to a valid contract quantity.
- maxQty optionally caps the resulting quantity. A quantity below one minimum contract is rejected.
- Stops use the sweep extreme plus an ATR buffer and must be inside the configured ATR range.
- Targets use the configured minimum and maximum RR bounds.
- Pyramiding is disabled and only one position or pending order is allowed.
- Optional breakeven moves the stop to entry after 1R.
- Optional trailing stop moves by trailR times the initial R after 1R.
- FVG limit orders expire after fvgExp bars; market entries expire after one bar if not filled.

HTF AND REPAINTING POLICY
-------------------------
- Daily and 4H requests use lookahead_off.
- HTF values are copied only on the first chart bar of a new HTF period, using the last completed HTF bar.
- Confirmed pivots are used only after their confirmation bars.
- Realtime and historical behavior still require verification in TradingView Bar Replay.
- Chart stops are not guaranteed maximum monetary losses because gaps, spread, slippage, contract rules,
  and broker execution can change realized results.

ALERTS
------
The strategy provides long and short alertcondition entries and structured order-fill alert messages.
Payload fields include strategy, version, symbol, timeframe, direction, entry, stop, and target.
No secrets are included. Alerts are notifications, not authorization to trade.
An external receiver must validate freshness, symbol permissions, duplicate signals, current position,
risk limits, and data quality before any broker action.

VISUALS AND DIAGNOSTICS
-----------------------
The script can display EMA21, EMA50, EMA200, daily VWAP and bands, 4H range and golden pocket,
previous-day high/low, order blocks, FVG/IFVG boxes, sweep labels, spike labels, VMC, tables, and debug data.
Turning visual display off does not disable the underlying setup logic.

BACKTESTING AND VALIDATION
--------------------------
The strategy must be compiled and tested in TradingView Strategy Tester using the same symbol/feed,
timezone, timeframe, session, and contract settings intended for evaluation.
Use Bar Replay around daily and 4H boundaries. Review expectancy, drawdown, average R, trade count,
slippage sensitivity, gaps, limit fills, same-bar stop/target ordering, and out-of-sample periods.
No profitability claim is made.

Local validation completed:

- TradingView compilation and Strategy Tester execution were not available locally.

SECURITY AND DEPLOYMENT
-----------------------
Do not put API keys, webhook secrets, or account credentials in Pine source or alert payloads.
Use paper alerts and paper execution before any separately authorized live deployment.
Keep a versioned copy of every deployed script and monitor alert delivery, symbol/timeframe,
position state, rejected orders, and data freshness.

LIMITATIONS
-----------
ICT terminology is represented by deterministic approximations and is not an objective market guarantee.
The script has no persistent backend, order reconciliation, broker adapter, kill switch, portfolio exposure
controller, account-equity sizing, or durable restart recovery.
Yahoo trial data previously downloaded for validation was removed from this workspace as requested.
